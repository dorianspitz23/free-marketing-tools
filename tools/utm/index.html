Checks

    All clear: lowercase, no spaces, nothing to encode.

    Why the checks matter. GA4 reports “Newsletter” and “newsletter” as two different sources, so one campaign quietly splits into several rows. Spaces turn into %20 or +, and characters like & or # can cut the link short.

    GA4 sorts visits into channels by medium. A medium it doesn’t recognise, like “newsletter” or “facebook”, lands in Unassigned. The check uses Google’s own default channel rules. Stick to lowercase words joined by hyphens.

    One per line: url, source, medium, campaign, then term and content if you use them. Commas or tabs, so you can paste straight from a spreadsheet. A header row is skipped.
