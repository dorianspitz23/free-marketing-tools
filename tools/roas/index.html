How the maths works. Price = order value minus discount. Profit per order before ads = (1 − return rate) × (price − product cost) − shipping − payment fee − return rate × cost per return. Returned orders are refunded in full and go back on the shelf; you keep paying the shipping, the fee and the return label.

Break-even ROAS = price ÷ profit per order. Target ROAS = price ÷ (profit per order − your profit goal × price). In Google Ads, enter the target as a percentage (3.5× = 350%). If your conversion value includes tax or shipping, your real target is a little higher.
