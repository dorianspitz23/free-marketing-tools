    Why pixels, not characters. Google cuts titles and descriptions when they run out of room on the screen, so a title full of wide letters like W and M gets cut sooner than one full of i and l. This tool measures your text in Google’s own font and size.

    The limits are estimates. Google doesn’t publish them and tests new layouts all the time. The tool uses the widths SEO tools commonly measure: about 600px for a desktop title, 920px for a desktop description and 680px on mobile. Ads drop whole headlines that don’t fit instead of cutting them.
