Merchant Center feed checker

Drop in a product feed and every row Google would disapprove or limit turns red, with the reason in plain English, before you upload it.

Paste CSV or tab-separated text with a header row. Columns can be in any order.

Read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Result

    Products

    Coral means Google rejects it. A missing required attribute, a wrong format or a duplicate id can get the product disapproved. Amber means it weakens the listing. It can still run, but it may be cut off or read as spam.

    Rules follow Google’s Merchant Center help pages. Requirements vary by country and category, and the list of promotional phrases and the ALL-CAPS check are estimates, since Google reviews those by judgement.
